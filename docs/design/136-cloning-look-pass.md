# 136. The Cloning tab, looked at in a browser

Status: the Cloning part of the look pass of item 133 (#398), built. Items
134 and 135 are the Enzymes and CRISPR passes.

What was asked: the same look as the other two, on the Cloning tab (the
Digest, PCR and Mutate bodies and the doors under them), with pBR322 open
and every enzyme that cuts it ticked, in the dark theme on a 1489 px
window. The Cloning tab has no workspace of its own (item 133, D-decision
on the Bench), so there are no pages to look at beside it.

## What was seen, and what was done about it

- **A status line that was a paragraph.** With the 35 enzymes pBR322 ticks
  by default the Digest's one status line listed every name and ran to
  five lines, which is the opposite of what item 133's slimming was for.
  Past four enzymes it names the first four and counts the rest ("AatII,
  AlwNI, AseI, AvaI and 28 more: 32 fragments, largest first."). The
  tutorials' lines name two enzymes or fewer, so none of them changed.

Seen and fine: the two-line fragment rows with Add and Open on the first
line (the range and features are in the row's tooltip), **All fragments
and partial digest ›** under them, the shelf line and the two doors
(**Open the Bench ›**, **Clone an insert into this plasmid ›**); the PCR
body with its two primer boxes, the closed **Options** drawer with its
summary ("Proofreading") and its one-sentence empty state; Mutate, which
was not touched.

## Seen and left

- The fragment rows keep their own scroll box inside the panel, which is
  a second scroll bar beside the sidebar's. It is the row window's box and
  the rows are many; left.
- The doors are plain links at the foot of the tab, below the shelf line.
  They read as links and are found; whether they should sit nearer the top
  is a question for someone using the tab for real.

## Not checked

The slim tab at 320 px and on the phone (the Details strip of six subtabs
is #405), and the light theme.
