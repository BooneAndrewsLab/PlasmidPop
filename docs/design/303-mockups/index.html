#303 · restriction-cloning ends on the primer workspace's Design page · mockups

Static HTML, no dependencies, the #300 mockups' tokens, frame, rail and tables reused (open them side by side: 300-mockups). Each file has a desktop frame (1250 × 900: the cloning page is taller than the plain one), a states section and a phone frame (390 px). The theme toggle follows the app's tokens; the rail switches pages inside a frame.

  1. design-cloning.html — Design in cloning mode: the mode switch, step 1 the vector region (pUC19 MCS 396–454 on the live map, blown up as a ruler), step 2 the insert (a feature of another tab, or Gene from NCBI… with the shared search collapsed, open and with a hit picked), step 3 the suggested enzyme pairs as a dense table with the excluded ones greyed and the Any two enzymes… override, step 4 the Design button and its three disabled-with-reason states, plus the plain mode's disabled button.
  2. results-cloning.html — Results for cloning primers: the table with padding / site / annealing told apart in each oligo, the annealing Tm, the warnings column; the detail pane with enzymes, padding, overhangs, warnings and the actions; the simulated product as a thumbnail; two warning states (internal site + methylation, Type IIS + vector cut outside the region).

Sample data

Vector pUC19 (L09137, 2,686 bp), region = the MCS 396–454: EcoRI 396, SacI 402, KpnI 408, SmaI 412, BamHI 417, XbaI 423, SalI 429, PstI 435, SphI 441, HindIII 447 (site starts; the table shows the cut position on the top strand). Insert = S. cerevisiae ACT1 CDS, 1,131 bp incl. the stop, from a second tab; the primers' annealing parts are the ORF's real ends. The insert's internal HindIII and KpnI sites, which grey every pair using them, are mock positions (872, 1,018): realistic for the gene, not checked against the record. Padding is 6 nt (gcatcg), raised to 8 for SalI. The product is pUC19-ACT1, 3,802 bp (2,686 − 21 bp dropped between the EcoRI and BamHI cuts + 1,137 bp of insert with its two overhang ends). Tm numbers are the #300 mockup's placeholder formula, not the app's.

What the mockups decided (proposals, for the maintainer to accept or change)
Where the mode lives A two-way switch at the top of the Design page, above the target chooser: Primers on this sequence / Clone an insert into this plasmid. The rail, Results, Check and My primers are unchanged; Results shows whichever table the last design made. One page, one place to look; the issue's "a cloning mode on the Design page". A fifth rail page would duplicate the settings and the results table.
Step order 1 vector region, 2 insert, 3 enzymes, 4 design — numbered, each with its value on one line. Steps 1 and 2 can be done in either order; step 3 fills itself once both are known. The enzymes depend on both the region (cut once inside it) and the insert (no internal site), so they come third and are suggested, not asked.
The pair table Dense, radio-picked, usable pairs first in the engine's rank (two sticky ends, then the wider gap between the cuts), excluded pairs greyed with the reason, the tail folded behind "N more excluded pairs". Columns: enzymes, cut positions, ends, directional, insert, host (methylation), why excluded. The reasons are the user's check on the engine; hiding excluded pairs entirely would make a "why not EcoRI · HindIII?" question unanswerable.
Override Any two enzymes…: two selects from the full list (all enzymes that cut the vector, not just the region's), including excluded pairs. Design then runs and warns; it never refuses. A partial digest or a different vector prep are the user's business; the warnings and the simulated product show the consequence.
Orientation Default: the insert's start (its 5′ end as a feature) goes to the lower-position enzyme, so the insert reads in the vector's forward direction; a Flip button in step 2 swaps the two sites between the primers. Needed for pUC19, where lacZα and the lac promoter run on the reverse strand: a fusion wants the other way round.
Results rows The picked pair first, then the other suggested pairs designed with the same settings (capped at six). Selecting a row shows its detail and its simulated product. Comparing EcoRI · BamHI with EcoRI · PstI is a click rather than a second design; the alternatives cost nothing to compute.
Oligo display Tail in lower case: padding in the muted ink, the site in the cut colour and underlined, the annealing part in upper case. Copy and the CSV write the same casing. Matches the issue ("tail in lower case") and the PCR panel's reading of a tailed primer; the site stands out when checking an order form.
Simulate A primary action in the detail pane: PCR with the two primers on the insert's tab, digest with the two enzymes, ligation into the vector's backbone, the product opened as a tab (named vector-insert). The thumbnail in the pane is the same product drawn small, so what it shows is what Simulate opens. The issue's "as In-Fusion's Open product does"; the thumbnail answers "did the insert land in the right place and direction" before a tab is opened.
Settings The same disclosure as the plain mode; Tm, annealing length, ΔTm and GC clamp are shared, padding and junction (avoid making a new site where padding, site and insert meet) are cloning-only and shown only in this mode. One settings memory, no second set of Tm limits to drift.
Warnings In the table as tags, in the detail pane as a list with the sentence that explains it: internal site in the insert, vector cut 0 or more than once, methylation (a site blocked on the vector, or a GATC / CCWGG made at a junction), incompatible or non-directional ends, Type IIS placement, padding raised for an enzyme that cuts poorly near an end, a new site made at the junction. Danger (the clone will be wrong) is red, caution amber. The issue's list plus the two that drawing the primers surfaced (junction sites, padding minimum).

Open questions for the maintainer
Default padding length 6 nt, as a setting (cloning-only), with a small per-enzyme minimum table for the few enzymes known to cut poorly near an end (NEB's "cleavage close to the end" list: SalI, NotI, XhoI, NdeI, SacII…), raised silently to that minimum and said in the warnings column ("padding 8 nt: SalI cuts poorly within 6 nt of an end"). The padding bases are chosen to make no new site with the site and the insert's first bases (the junction setting). 4 nt (shorter oligos, but under several enzymes' minimum); a fixed per-enzyme value with no setting; the user types the padding.
Vector region when nothing is selected Fall back in this order, saying which applies on the step 1 line: the selection; else a feature typed or named as the MCS (misc_feature "MCS", "multiple cloning site", "polylinker") if the document has exactly one; else the whole plasmid, with the suggestions limited to enzymes that cut once anywhere and a hint "select a region to narrow". Never silently pick a region the user did not see. Require a selection (Design disabled with "Select a region of the vector", the fourth reason state in the mockup) — simplest, and the strongest candidate if the MCS-feature guess feels like magic.
Non-directional single-enzyme cloning in v1 No. Same-enzyme and blunt · blunt pairs are greyed as "not directional"; the override still allows them and Design warns ("the insert can go in either way; dephosphorylate the vector and screen the orientation"). A proper single-enzyme flow (one site on both primers, −P on the vector, both orientations simulated) is its own issue. Allow it in v1 with the warning only (cheap, since the override already exists) — acceptable if the maintainer prefers fewer greyed rows.
Insert from NCBI: a tab, or held in the workspace? Fetched as a tab, as note 107 settled for the plain mode's gene target, with its CDS feature (the Use select: CDS, mRNA, or the gene with flanks) offered as the insert. The workspace then holds a reference (tab + feature), exactly as for a feature of any other open tab, so the one code path serves both sources. Simulate's PCR needs a template document anyway, and the product's "made from" record points at it. Hold the record in the workspace only and open a tab on Simulate — one fewer tab, but two insert kinds in the engine and nothing on screen to check the gene against.
Alternatives in Results Design the picked pair and up to five other usable pairs with the same settings; the picked one is row 1. The Design primers button says which pair it is for. Only the picked pair (one row; Results is then a detail pane with a table of one).
Type IIS enzymes Not in the suggestions in v1 (Golden Gate is out of scope); allowed through the override, with the placement warning the second warning state shows and the site placed so the overhang is the insert's own bases. Refuse them in the override too, until a Golden Gate designer exists.
Page height The cloning Design page is taller than the plain one (four steps plus a table): the page scrolls inside the workspace, the pair table folds its excluded tail and caps at ~8 rows before scrolling. On a window ≤ 500 px high the steps' sub-lines hide. Fold steps 1 and 2 to one line each once set (a wizard look); rejected because the table is where the user's attention goes and the lines above it must stay readable.
What the map shows Design: the region as the selection band and the picked pair's two cuts in the cut colour (the other enzymes' marks follow the Cut sites toggle as usual). Results: the same; the product is a thumbnail in the pane and a tab after Simulate, never drawn over the vector. Draw the insert on the vector's map as a preview arc — tempting, but the positions past the insert shift and the map would lie.
Saved primer names Save both names them insert-F-enzyme / insert-R-enzyme (ACT1-F-EcoRI, ACT1-R-BamHI), with the notes line "ACT1 CDS into pUC19 · padding 6". Ask for names (a dialog) — the plain mode does not, so neither should this.
