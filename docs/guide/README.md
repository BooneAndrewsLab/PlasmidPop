# PlasmidPop user guide

The same pages open inside the app from the **?** button at the right of the
toolbar, where a search box above the page list searches all of them. They are
also published as plain web pages, readable without JavaScript, at `/guide/` on
the PlasmidPop site, with the same search box once their script has loaded.

Step-by-step exercises in cloning and sequence analysis, with sample files to download, are the
[tutorials](../tutorials/README.md), in `docs/tutorials` and at `/tutorials/` on the site.

1. [Getting started](01-getting-started.md)
2. [Files and storage](02-files.md): formats, opening several files or a ZIP, opening a record from NCBI, document tabs, saving, comparing with another file or tab, sharing a link, exporting, recent files, backing up and restoring everything, usage statistics, offline use, opening an attachment from another app
3. [Viewing and selecting](03-viewing.md): sequence view, circular map, GC content, selection, previews, on a phone
4. [Editing the sequence](04-editing.md): typing, copy and paste, colouring and enlarging bases, reverse complement, origin, edit marks, undo
5. [Features](05-features.md): the feature list and editor, detecting common features by their bases and by what they code for, and My parts, your own parts for it to look for
6. [Find](06-find.md): a sequence, an ambiguity code or a feature name, matches shown on the map and in the sequence view
7. [Restriction enzymes](07-enzymes.md): the slim tab and the Enzyme workspace; the enzyme table and what to show, cut sites, a drawn gel and diagnostic digests, isoschizomers, importing REBASE, Dam/Dcm methylation, adding your own enzyme
8. [Open reading frames](08-orfs.md): finding ORFs by minimum length and genetic code, copying the protein, adding one as a CDS feature
9. [Translation](09-translate.md): under CDS features and in six frames, recoding a CDS for a host, residues numbered
10. [Primers](10-primers.md): the slim tab and the primer workspace; design, preview and checking; a batch of regions or point mutations designed from a pasted table, with a pool check and an order-sheet CSV; My primers, a list kept in the browser, and finding where its primers bind
11. [Alignment and assembly](11-align.md): a slim Align tab and an Align workspace beside the map; pairwise alignment of a read or a batch of reads against the document, with qualities; reads assembled into a consensus, with read pairs found by name to place reads in a repeat and flag a contig that lost a stretch; a plate of clone consensuses verified against the expected constructs; three to fifty sequences aligned together, with a consensus, exported as aligned FASTA or Clustal, from the Align tab or from File ▸ Multiple alignment… with no document open
12. [Simulated cloning](12-cloning.md): digest, PCR and site-directed mutagenesis in the Cloning tab; the Bench, with the shelf, ligation, Golden Gate, modular standards (MoClo, YTK) with combinatorial plans, Gibson, overlap-extension PCR, Gateway and yeast assembly; the check digest against the empty vector; what a product was made from; a bench protocol for a product
13. [History](13-history.md): what a product was made from, the list of changes, jumping between them, naming a state, what one step changed, and keeping it across reloads
14. [Keyboard shortcuts](14-shortcuts.md), and putting an action on another key
15. [Sequencing reads](15-reads.md): AB1 and FASTQ files, qualities and traces, export as FASTQ
16. [Proteins](16-proteins.md): protein FASTA and GenPept, Open as protein, weight, pI and extinction coefficient, back-translation
17. [CRISPR guides](17-crispr.md): the slim tab and the CRISPR workspace; guide sites for SpCas9, SaCas9, Cas12a or a custom PAM, off-targets in the open documents, flags, base-editing windows, pegRNAs, paired nickases, cloning oligos
18. [Validation](18-validation.md): what is cross-checked against which independent oracle and with which version, the number of cases, the known differences, and the latest mutation score, all generated from the committed data when the app is built
